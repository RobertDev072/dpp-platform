The digital product passport is not arriving for all products at once. The EU works **per product group**. This overview shows where each group stands (October 2026).

## Already fixed

**Batteries.** For EV batteries, light means of transport batteries (e-bikes, e-scooters) and industrial batteries above 2 kWh, the passport is mandatory from **18 February 2027**. Read more in [The battery passport from 18 February 2027](/en/blog/battery-passport-2027).

## Priorities in the ESPR working plan 2025–2030

The European Commission adopted the working plan in April 2025. These groups come first:

| Product group | Expected delegated act |
|---|---|
| Iron and steel | 2026 |
| Aluminium | 2027 |
| Textiles (mainly apparel) | 2027 |
| Tyres | 2027 |
| Furniture | 2028 |
| Mattresses | 2028–2029 |

After adoption there are, as a rule, **at least 18 months** before the requirements apply. **Horizontal requirements** will also follow, for example on the repairability of electronics.

## Through other legislation

- **Construction products**: the new Construction Products Regulation (EU) 2024/3110 introduces the digital product passport in phases, per product family.
- **Toys**: the new EU toy safety rules include a digital product passport, with a transition period of several years.

## What does this mean?

Even if your product group is a few years away: **collecting data from suppliers takes time**. Companies that centralise their product data now will be ready in days instead of months.
